# The World in One Day — Sunday, 22 November 2026

A newspaper front page that sums up the world's news of Sunday,
22 November 2026, drawn with Python (PIL) in `gen_image.py`. This is a
[make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122) entry for the
`news-image-20261122` bounty. `main.py` (standard library only) copies
`entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.

## The four stories

- **COP31 collapses in Antalya.** The climate summit ended on Sunday
  with no deal: the EU and about 130 nations rejected a final text
  that never mentioned fossil fuels.
- **Drone strike in Dymer, Ukraine.** A Russian Shahed drone hit a
  five-storey apartment block at 5 a.m.; 14 people were missing, and
  7-year-old David was pulled alive from the rubble after 14 hours.
- **Russian warship sunk off Libya.** Suspected Ukrainian sea drones
  hit the corvette Merkury at Benghazi naval base and it capsized;
  a second ship was hit off the coast that evening.
- **Democrats win 258 House seats.** Their largest US House majority
  since 1992, sealed when Chuck Hubbard beat Rep. Virginia Foxx in
  North Carolina after a recount.

Also today: BepiColombo reaches Mercury after eight years (ESA reported
a thruster glitch); the search goes on at the Fonusa candy factory in
Cikande, Indonesia; Peru's Sunday toll from Friday's 6.7 Junin quake.
